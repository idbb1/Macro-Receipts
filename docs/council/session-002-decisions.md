# Council Session 002 — Synthesis and Decisions

**Date:** 28–29 August 2026
**Question:** "We have zero following. If we post a receipt, maybe 100 people will see it even if
we include hashtags. How do we get attention?"
**Input:** five independent contributions, [`session-002-brainstorm.md`](./session-002-brainstorm.md)

---

## The headline

The council spent most of its answer on the premise rather than the tactics, because the premise is
wrong in a load-bearing way:

> **Follower count gates your own posts. It does not gate your replies and quote-tweets, which are
> ranked inside someone else's audience.**

X retrieves out-of-network candidates by content similarity and ranks them on predicted engagement —
replies weighted above likes, dwell and profile-clicks counted, outbound links that bounce counted
against you. Follower count is an input to almost none of it. So a standalone post from this account
reaches 20–90 people, but a reply placed early under a large macro account is ranked inside *that*
post's pool and routinely does orders of magnitude more.

Two seats reached this independently and stated it in nearly the same words. It reframes the problem
from *"how do we get reach"* to *"what is the cheapest at-bat, and how many can one person generate
per day."*

**On the hashtags: all five seats said delete them, permanently.** They stopped being a retrieval
path on X years ago, two or more correlates with spam patterns, and — the sharper point — the only
accounts still using them are accounts with no distribution, so they actively signal that you have
none. Removing them is free and strictly positive.

**On "100 people": four of five said that is optimistic**, not pessimistic. The realistic floor for
a cold standalone post is 20–90 impressions, mostly bots. The good news is not that the number is
higher than you think. It is that the number does not matter, because standalone posting is not the
mechanism.

## Three mechanisms — sequenced, not competing

The seats appeared to propose three different plans. They are three layers of one plan, and they
run concurrently on different clocks.

### Layer 1 — The reply engine (the daily grind)

Endorsed by four seats, opposed by one. The account plants receipts under other people's
already-ranked posts rather than firing them into an empty field. Every macro print generates,
within ten minutes, a set of large posts that are pure assertion with no record attached — a
distribution asset already loaded with exactly the right audience and with an obvious hole in it.

The constraint every supporting seat attached independently: **the reply is a sourced artefact and
nothing else.** No adjectives, no verdict of your own, no tag, no link, no emoji. Session 001's
no-takes rule holds *harder* at zero, because at zero a take is all anyone has to judge you by.

Hard rule from the Distribution seat: **be in the first ten replies or skip it.** A reply at minute
forty is a message in a bottle. This is why the pre-rendered ammunition and the resolution calendar
have to exist before the loop starts — you cannot source and render a receipt at minute eight of a
live thread.

### Layer 2 — The gift to the audience-owner (the highest-leverage play)

**This is the strongest convergence in the session.** Three seats proposed it independently, in
three different framings, and none had seen the others:

| Seat | Framing | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | "Do not publish. Deliver." A gift with no ask, hand-addressed | 25 podcast producers, starved of segment prep |
| Funnel | The **Guest Ledger** — a show's last 40 guests, graded, with the show's own aggregate on the cover | 40 shows, ranked by roster overlap |
| Psychology | A printed, numbered ledger, physically mailed, right-of-reply before publication | The 12 forecasters and 15 named journalists |

All three are the same mechanism: **produce a document about someone else's audience, give it to
them free with no ask, and let them distribute it.** It works because it is about them, because it
costs them nothing, because it makes them look good to their own audience, and because it carries
*their* standing rather than asking for yours.

The Funnel seat's version is the most operational and the cheapest to produce — it is one query
over a dataset being built anyway, grouped by show instead of by person. No host has ever had a
number for their own programme. Adopt that as the primary form, delivered physically to the top 25
(Psychology's contribution) and framed as a gift with no ask (Positioning's).

### Layer 3 — The wrong audience first

Two seats, independently: go to the forecasting-and-epistemics crowd before macro X — Metaculus,
Manifold, the Astral Codex Ten open thread, r/slatestarcodex, a Show HN for the CC-BY dataset and
the published rules. That community already believes scoring forecasters is the highest calling,
has no macro data, will audit the method for free, and is read by people with large macro
followings. It is small, uncontested, and methodologically sympathetic — the cheapest standing
available anywhere.

## The real conflict: standing versus at-bats

This is the sharpest disagreement the council has produced.

**Psychology's position:** the owner has diagnosed the wrong disease. This is not a reach problem,
it is a **standing** problem, and reach is what would make it fatal. A zero-follower account posting
a graded verdict on a famous investor does not read as an audit; it reads as a stranger awarding
marks to a billionaire. The artefact is identical and the reading is set entirely by the perceived
standing of the source. Impressions are not neutral: everyone who files you under "anonymous dunk
account" has filed you permanently, and there is no appeals process for a category error.

**Reduction's position:** an institution with zero readers is a diary. Scarcity and indifference are
strategies for someone who already has attention. Adopt them at 5,000 followers, not at zero.

**Resolution — both are right about different things, and one idea dissolves most of it.**

The Positioning seat supplied it without framing it as a resolution: **grade the Fed first.** Receipt
#0001 is the December 2024 SEP dots against the actual 2025 path. It is precisely dated, free,
already resolved, the most-argued-about forecast in macro — and no human being is humiliated by it.
It proves the method on a subject nobody can accuse you of bullying, which is exactly the standing
problem Psychology identified, solved at zero cost before a single named person is graded.

Beyond that: what would spend standing is a *take* or a *dunk*, not a citation. A reply that is
nothing but a sourced quote, a date and a price does not read as a stranger with opinions — it reads
as someone holding a document. So the reply engine runs, and the content rule is what protects the
posture. Psychology's parcel play buys standing in parallel, on a separate clock.

**What Psychology wins outright:** publish the House row — our own graded record, currently losing —
before grading anyone else, and have the Contested page live *before* the first dispute rather than
after. Both are cheap and both pre-empt the only strong attack line.

## Amendment to Session 001: the tag/QT distinction

Session 001 banned tagging. The Distribution seat has drawn a distinction that survives scrutiny and
amends that ruling:

- **A tag (`@handle` in the body) is a solicitation.** It reads as *someone wants my retweet*, and
  the asymmetry is worst at zero followers, where it is visibly begging. **Banned permanently.**
- **A quote-tweet of the forecaster's own post containing the call is a citation.** Ratings agencies
  do not @ you, but they do quote your filings verbatim with a reference number. Session 001's own
  logic — an indifferent institution reaching a conclusion — is preserved by the QT and destroyed by
  the tag. **Permitted.**
- **Replies under their posts** attach the handle automatically, so it is not a choice. **Permitted.**

Banning the QT as well would ban distribution itself, and there would be no cold start.

**Attached condition, from the same seat and adopted:** the **symmetry rule.** If you QT someone's
MISS, you QT their next HIT with identical framing and equal prominence — and you publish the ratio.

## The decided plan

**Now (this week)**
1. Delete hashtags from every template, permanently.
2. Buy X Premium. $8/month. It weights replies, which is the entire mechanism — the one paid input
   that multiplies the one thing done 140 times a week.
3. Account live with the pinned artefact **before** any distribution exists. Every reply's conversion
   runs through the profile, and an empty profile converts at zero.
4. Build the resolution calendar — every resolved and soon-to-resolve call mapped to the print that
   resolves it. A spreadsheet, four hours, not a system.
5. Pre-render 25 receipt cards from already-resolved calls. Ammunition before the fight.
6. Build the 40-account watchlist (large macro accounts, *not* the scored forecasters) and the Dream
   100 sheet (shows ranked by roster overlap, not by size).

**Weeks 1–4**
7. Reply loop starts immediately, using pre-rendered stock, before receipt #1 publishes.
8. Receipt #0001 is the Fed's own dots. The House row publishes alongside it. Contested page live.
9. Three Guest Ledgers produced by hand for the three shows most likely to publish — independent,
   personality-led, no network legal department.
10. Ledger #1 to one friendly host with zero ask. Fix what breaks, then send the rest.

**Weeks 5–12**
11. Ledgers in weekly batches. Physical mail to the top 25.
12. CC-BY dataset into the epistemics communities and ten quant accounts.
13. Twelve forecaster cards by private DM — after there is a body of published work, not before.
14. Only then start the weekly ritual. Nobody notices a rhythm they cannot yet hear.

**The metric while distribution is being found:** profile visits per day, or profile clicks per 100
replies. Session 001 said ignore followers and impressions — correct at scale, wrong at zero, and
the Distribution seat was right to push back. You cannot navigate without instruments. Receipts per
week remains the production metric; it is not yet the distribution metric.

## Money

Total across every seat's recommendation: **roughly $3,000, and $500 of it is genuinely optional.**

| Item | Cost | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| X Premium | $8–40/mo | Reduction, Distribution |
| Print and mail the ledgers | $450–1,500 | Funnel, Psychology |
| ~40 hours of research time to build receipt inventory | ~$2,000 | Positioning |
| Email list | ~$300/yr | Positioning |
| **Advertising** | **$0** | **All five, explicitly** |

The unanimous reasoning on ads: you cannot buy your way into an interest graph, and paid impressions
arriving before credibility buy the single worst commodity available to this project. As the
Psychology seat put it, money spent on paper is visible and money spent on impressions signals
nothing.

## Unanimous do-not list

Hashtags · advertising · engagement pods · bought followers · follow-for-follow · dunking, ever ·
tagging in the body · a waitlist · a second platform before distribution is found on the first ·
threads about methodology before there are receipts to attach them to.

**And a reversal worth noting.** In session 001 the Distribution seat argued for "document, don't
create" — the build-in-public story as free content. At zero followers it reversed itself, and three
seats now agree: **do not build in public.** The founder-journey narrative is standard advice and it
is wrong here, because the asset is institutional indifference. A build-in-public account has a
protagonist, and you cannot be simultaneously the referee and the underdog.

## Where the seats genuinely disagree, unresolved

**The arithmetic gap is 5×, and the synthesis should not paper over it.** Both seats that modelled
the reply engine produced numbers, and they are far apart:

| Seat | Followers per reply | 90-day projection from replies |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction | ~1.0 after a 60% haircut | ~1,200 from 1,200 replies |
| Distribution | ~0.2 blended | 480–600 from ~2,700 replies |

Use the lower one for planning. It comes from the seat whose whole discipline is this mechanism, it
is the more conservative, and the difference is not a rounding error — one model says the grind
alone clears 1,000 and the other says it gets you halfway. Both agree the tail matters more than the
mean: roughly one post in 250 finds real distribution, so the grind's real job is buying enough
lottery tickets that the breakout happens this quarter instead of next year.

**Volume is disputed too.** Reduction and Distribution want 20–30 replies a day. Positioning wants 20
a *week*, each carrying a card, on the grounds that twenty replies with documents beat two hundred
with personality. Funnel opposes the reply engine outright as building an audience that came for
takes. Start at 20/day and cut if the copy drifts toward commentary — the failure mode Positioning
and Funnel are pointing at is real, and it shows up in the copy before it shows up in the numbers.

**The honest risk, and the most useful sentence the council produced:** the modal outcome for
someone who commits to 30 replies a day is roughly 110 followers at day 90 — because they did it for
nine days and then decided the product needed more work. **The risk here is not the algorithm.**
